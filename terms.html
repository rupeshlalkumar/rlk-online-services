Terms of Service & Guidelines

Guidelines regarding service requests, data verification, transparent quotes, and customer obligations.

1. Nature of Services

RLK Online Services operates as an independent cyber cafe and online service facilitation centre. We assist clients with online portal navigation, data entry, scanning, image resizing, document conversion, printing, business profile creation, and English study guidance. We are not a government agency, nor do we issue official certificates directly; certificates, receipts, and approvals are issued exclusively by the respective competent authorities.

2. Client Verification Responsibility

Clients are solely responsible for ensuring that all information provided (including candidate name spellings, parent names, date of birth, category, academic marks, and account details) is authentic and accurate. Before final submission of any online exam, admission, or government form, a review draft is presented to the client for verification.

3. Pricing & Transparent Quotes

In accordance with our business policy, service prices are not published on the public website because individual requirements vary based on complexity, number of pages, urgency, and portal payment challans. Exact quotes combining statutory portal fees and our nominal facilitation charge are provided transparently upon WhatsApp or in-person consultation prior to work commencement.

4. Server Uptime & Portal Deadlines

While we make every effort to submit applications well before deadlines, third-party government and university servers are subject to high traffic, downtime, or maintenance. We strongly recommend that clients contact us well in advance of official closing dates.

5. Revisions and Corrections

If a government or examination authority opens an official correction window, we gladly assist candidates in modifying allowed fields during that period.

6. Contact & Queries
